Technology Architecture

Multimodal AI for Document Intelligence

Three state-of-the-art models working in concert—a unique architecture deployed by Privara Labs

01

TrOCR: Transformer OCR

Microsoft's state-of-the-art optical character recognition extracts text from printed and handwritten documents with 97% accuracy across 50+ languages including Hindi, Tamil, Arabic, and Chinese.

  • Multilingual text extraction
  • Handwriting recognition
  • Key-value pair detection
02

YOLO: Visual Detection

Ultralytics YOLOv8 identifies photographs, signatures, seals, and visual PII at 60 FPS with sub-second latency for enterprise-scale document processing workflows.

  • Real-time photo detection
  • Signature identification
  • Seal and stamp recognition
03

LayoutLMv3: Document Intelligence

Microsoft's layout understanding model links field labels to values in complex forms—the critical capability missing from traditional redaction tools.

  • Form structure analysis
  • Table extraction
  • Context-aware redaction

Key Privacy Differentiators

While competitors rely on single OCR engines achieving 70-80% accuracy, Privara combines three specialized models for 95%+ accuracy. Our system detects both field labels AND values—essential for banking KYC, medical records, and research data compliance.
